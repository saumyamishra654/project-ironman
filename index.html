Sleep protocol Wake 06:30 · Lights out 22:30 · 8 h in bed
  1. 06:30WakeSame time every day, weekends ±30 min. Outdoor light within an hour (morning run/swim covers it).
  2. 12:00Caffeine cutoffIncludes chai, green tea, dark chocolate.
  3. 18:00Lift (Mon/Wed/Fri)Done by 18:45. Hard cardio stays in the morning.
  4. 19:30Dinner doneMost filling meal: protein + veg + slow carbs. Don't take the day's deficit out of dinner.
  5. 20:30Taper fluidsMost water before this, to avoid waking up to pee.
  6. 21:30Screens off · magnesium · optional snackDim lights. Read, brush, prep oats, podcasts. Snack from the list below, if hungry.
  7. 22:15In bedBook or audio only. Phone out of reach.
  8. 22:30Lights outCool, dark, quiet room.
Pre-bed snacks (~150–250 kcal, mostly protein, ~21:30)
Go-to
  • Greek yogurt / hung curd
  • Amul HP milk / a glass of milk
  • Dahi (plain)
  • Paneer, a few cubes
  • 1–2 boiled eggs
  • Chia pudding made with milk
Fine, keep it small
  • Blueberries / berries (a handful, ideally with yogurt)
  • Kiwi (1–2), half a banana
  • Almonds / walnuts (~15–20 g)
  • Peanut butter (1 tbsp)
  • David bar (if it sits well; sweeteners bother some guts)
Avoid
  • Big carb meals, desserts, ice cream
  • Spicy / fried / heavy
  • Chai, coffee, dark chocolate
  • Alcohol (< 3 h before bed)
  • Big drinks (watermelon, juice)
