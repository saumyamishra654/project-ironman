## What the video says (and how it's used here)

| Nippard step | What he says | How this plan uses it |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Arch | Use at least a moderate arch. | Moderate arch with leg drive on all competition-style bench. |
| 1. Bar path | Lower the bar down and slightly forward, then press it **back toward your face, then up** (elite benchers do this; novices press straight up). Film from the side. | Cue: *"push the floor away, bar back then up."* Film the Tue top set from the side every week. Iron Path or similar optional. |
| 2. Wider grip | Widen your grip **about one finger per workout** over a few weeks, up to the rings if comfortable: shorter ROM, a stronger mid-range, a tighter upper back. | **About one finger per week** (you bench 3×/week, so per-workout would be too fast) for 3–4 weeks, until your index finger is near the rings. Stop widening if your shoulders object or the bar slows. |
| 3. Frequency | **3×/week is the sweet spot**, set up as daily undulating periodisation (DUP, each day a different focus): hypertrophy (sets of 8), power (light, fast), strength (heavy 5s). | Mon **Power** · Tue **Hypertrophy + top set** · Fri **Strength**. |
| 4. Heavy top set | **One top set a week at ~90% of 1RM for 2–3 reps**, then back-off work. Don't max out regularly. | Tue top set at RPE 8 before the 3×8 back-offs. |

**Pull-ups (my adaptation):** the same three day-types: weighted strength (Fri, fresh and first, as the split already has it), bodyweight volume (Tue), and light crisp sets (Mon).

## Phase A: Oct 19 – Dec 20 (Meso 1.1, maintenance calories, HM build)

### Weekly template

| Day | Bench | Pull-up | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** (Lower day, after legs) | **Power:** 6×3 at **65 kg** (~68%), 1-second pause on the chest, as fast as possible | **Light:** 3×6 bodyweight, crisp, full hang | ~15–20 min added to the Lower session. Legs stay first. |
| **Tue** (Upper A) | **Top set:** 1×2–3 at RPE 8 (start **85 kg**, ~89%) → **back-offs:** 3×8 at **72.5 kg** (~75%) | **Volume:** 4 sets bodyweight at RIR 2 (start ~9/8/8/7) | Replaces the feet-up bench (2 sets) and the lat pulldown (2 sets). Rows, close-grip and arms stay. |
| **Fri** (Upper B) | **Strength:** 4×5 at **77.5 kg** (~81%) | **Weighted strength, FIRST:** top set 1×4 at **+17.5 kg** (RPE 8) → 2×5 at **+12.5** | Order: pull-up → bench → OHP (2–3 sets) → accessories. Bench 4×5 replaces the incline DB press. |

Weekly volume: about 14 bench sets (6 of them light power sets) and about 10 pull-up sets on top of rows. This lands at the plan's 12–14 sets per muscle per week. If your lats or elbows feel the pull-ups on top of 2–3 swims, drop **Mon pull-ups first**.

### Progression rules

| Slot | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tue top set | 3 reps at **RPE ≤ 8** → +2.5 kg next week · RPE 9 → repeat · fewer than 3 reps or RPE ≥ 9.5 → −2.5 kg |
| Tue back-offs 3×8 | Double progression: 3×8 → 3×10 at the same load, then +2.5 kg and back to 3×8 |
| Fri 4×5 | All sets at RPE ≤ 8 → +2.5 kg · otherwise repeat |
| Mon power 6×3 | Load stays at ~65–70% of your current estimated 1RM. Bar speed is the target. +2.5 kg roughly every 3 weeks, and never grind a rep. |
| Fri weighted pull-up | Top set 3–5 reps at RPE 8. Hit 5 reps → +1.25–2.5 kg. Back-offs are always −5 kg. |
| Tue BW volume | Add 1–2 total reps per week. At 4×10, wear +5 kg and drop back to ~4×7. |
| Grip | +1 finger width per week, weeks 1–4, then hold. |

**Expected by Dec 20:** top set ~90×3 (estimated 1RM ~98–100), weighted pull-up ~+20×5, bodyweight max back to ~18. These are estimates at maintenance calories with HM training on top.

**HM week (Dec 14–20), deload:** Mon 4×3 power; Tue top set at RPE 7 plus 2×8; Fri off or 3×5 light. Race Sun Dec 20.

## Phase B: Dec 21 – Feb 14 (Meso 1.2 bench block, lean bulk, lift 4×)

The periodisation plan sets the frame: **5 weeks of volume → 2 weeks of heavy singles/doubles → test week.** The 4th lift day is an extra upper volume day.

| Weeks | Mon (Lower + bench) | Tue (Upper A) | Thu (Upper C, new) | Fri (Upper B) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1–5** · Dec 21 – Jan 24 | Power 6×3 (~67%) · pull-ups 3×6 | Top set 1×2–3 at RPE 8 (~90%) → **4×8** | Close-grip bench 3×8 · incline DB 3×10 · **BW pull-up volume 4 sets** · rows · arms | Weighted pull-up top set + 2 back-offs → **bench 5×5** (~80–82%) → OHP |
| **6–7** · Jan 25 – Feb 7 | Power 4×2 (~65%) | Work up to **1×1 at RPE 8 → 8.5–9** → 3×3 at ~85% | Volume cut by about half | Weighted pull-up **3RM** work → bench **4×2 at ~88–90%** |
| **8** · Feb 8–14, test | Power 3×2, light | **Test day** (or Wed): warm-up → 92.5 → 97.5 → **100**, then 102.5–105 if 100 moves well (take 97.5 → 100 if the opener is RPE 9+) | Off | Weighted pull-up 3RM test + BW max reps |

Rules carry over from Phase A, with two changes: back-offs go to 4×8 and the strength day to 5×5 while the surplus supports it.

**Targets:** bench **100 likely, 102.5–105 reach**. That's up from the periodisation plan's 95–97.5 / 100, which assumed an e1RM of ~91; it now matches the 100 floor / 105 reach in `CLAUDE.md`. Weighted pull-up **+20–22.5×5** (added-load 1RM about +33–36). Bodyweight max **18–20**.
