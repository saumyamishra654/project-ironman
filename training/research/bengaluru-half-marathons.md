### Likely Times venues, ranked (my inference)

1. **Kanteerava Stadium, central-business-district loop.** This is Bengaluru's standard central stadium base (Bengaluru Marathon, Dhash HM), it fits the Times pattern, and one listing names it. Proxy course: the Bengaluru Marathon half loop, **~131 m of climbing per ~20 km**, rolling, with a ~500 m incline toward Vidhana Soudha and an ~800 m incline on Kasturba Rd toward MG Road in km 12 ([geeks on feet 2025 preview](https://geeksonfeet.com/posts/bengaluru-marathon-2025-route-preview/)). The 2024 full marathon (two loops) measured 190 m ([2024 preview](https://geeksonfeet.com/posts/bengaluru-marathon-2024-route-preview/)).
2. **Whitefield (KTPO).** An out-and-back used by the Midnight Marathon; the area sits at ~874 m. No published profile.
3. **NICE Road (Hosakerehalli).** Unlikely on Dec 20, because Thump! is listed there that morning. NICE Road is much hillier: **115 m per 10 km** out-and-back (measured on the Namma 10K, Oct 11), i.e. ~11.5 m/km versus ~6.5 m/km on the central loop.

**Training implication:** prepare for a rolling city course, ~130 m over 21 km, with short climbs of 0.5–0.8 km. When the real route drops, rebuild the elevation profile and a power-based pacing plan.
