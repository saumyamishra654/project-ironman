# Cycling Progression: What 10 Years Could Look Like

*Research brief for Saumya, 12 October 2026. Covers how much a fit runner's fitness transfers to the bike, how fast novice cyclists improve, what trained amateurs reach, how FTP turns into a 70.3 bike split, and a lower-confidence 10-year FTP projection (age 22 → 32). Every number has a link. Lines marked "my arithmetic" or "estimate" are calculations or judgement, not cited facts.*

---

## Executive summary

1. **Fitness is sport-specific.** Runners reach a higher VO₂max on a treadmill than on a bike. Cyclists reach similar values on both. Submaximal adaptations such as ventilatory threshold are also specific, and more so in cycling ([Millet 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19290675/)). His running fitness won't fully carry over at first.
2. **Novices respond strongly to bike training.**
   - On cycle-ergometer training, mean VO₂max gain was about 400 mL/min in 20 weeks, from almost nothing to more than 1 L/min ([HERITAGE, Bouchard 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10484570/)).
   - A strenuous mix of bike intervals and running gave **+44% in 10 weeks** ([Hickson 1977](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/838658/)).
   - Every "non-responder" responded at a higher dose ([Montero & Lundby 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5451738/)).
3. **Volume largely sets the ceiling.**
   - Trained cyclists riding 8.2 ± 3.6 h/week had critical power **4.17 ± 0.60 W/kg**. Active but cycling-untrained men (VO₂max 53) had **3.19 ± 0.35 W/kg**. In the trained group, training volume correlated with critical power at **r = 0.86** ([trained vs untrained CP study, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7560916/)).
   - A future Grand Tour top-10 rider raised his record power at every duration from age 18 to 23 as annual volume rose 79% ([Pinot & Grappe 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25357188/)).
4. **The bike decides a 70.3.** Cycling is 50–60% of race time and the best predictor of the total (r = 0.85). FTP alone explained 63.8% of cycling time, and cycling training explained more than 50% of variance ([70.3 systematic review 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13379040/)).
5. **Race intensity:** practitioners recommend riding a 70.3 at about **75–87% of FTP**, lower for slower finishers. This comes from Allen & Coggan via [BestBikeSplit](https://www.bestbikesplit.com/blog/pacing-70-3-bike-leg-intensity-factor-tss) and is not peer-reviewed. Measured half-Ironman pacing is steadier than in shorter races; only about 21% of time was spent more than 10% above average power ([Wu 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25557388/)).
6. **Age:** no meaningful cycling decline before 32. Cycling performance holds up with age better than running and swimming in triathletes ([Lepers 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23797729/)).

## 2. Evidence

| Study | Finding |
|---|---|
| [Millet 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19290675/) | VO₂max is specific to the exercise mode. Runners score higher on the treadmill; cyclists score similarly on both. Specific submaximal gains such as ventilatory threshold can come without a VO₂max change, likely more so in cycling. |
| [Bouchard 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10484570/) (cycle training, n=481) | +~400 mL/min mean in 20 weeks; range near 0 to >1 L/min; heritability 47%. |
| [Hickson 1977](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/838658/) | Strenuous bike intervals plus runs: VO₂max +44% in 10 weeks, linear. |
| [Montero & Lundby 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5451738/) | Cycling max power (Wmax) non-response disappears with an extra 120 min/week. |
| [Trained vs untrained CP, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7560916/) | Trained (8.2 h/week): CP 301 W, 4.17 W/kg, VO₂max 63.7. Untrained but active: CP 242 W, 3.19 W/kg, VO₂max 53.4. Volume vs CP r = 0.86. |
| [Pinot & Grappe 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25357188/) | World-class rider from 18 to 23: annual training duration +79%, record power up at every duration from 5 min to 4 h. |
| [Leo 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8783871/) | Narrative review of power-profiling methods. Field testing should follow standardised protocols. |
| [Borszcz 2018](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325567458_Functional_Threshold_Power_in_Cyclists_Validity_of_the_Concept_and_Physiological_Responses) | Examines whether FTP is a valid threshold concept. Treat FTP as a practical marker, not an exact lactate threshold. |
| [70.3 systematic review 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13379040/) | Cycling is 50–60% of duration, r = 0.85 with total time; FTP predicts 63.8% of cycling time; age-groupers train 6.5–11.5 h/week. |
| [Wu 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25557388/) | Half-Ironman bike pacing is more even than in sprint or Olympic distance. |
| [Lepers 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23797729/) | Age and sex effects in triathlon differ by discipline; cycling declines least with age. |

## 3. Conflicts and weak evidence

- **FTP and W/kg categories** (the Coggan power profile, "untrained 1.5–2.5 W/kg", and so on) are **practitioner tables**, not peer-reviewed distributions. The peer-reviewed anchors here are the trained/untrained CP study and HERITAGE.
- **No longitudinal study tracks amateur FTP over 5–10 years.** The long tail is inferred from the strong volume–CP relationship.
- **70.3 intensity factor** comes from practitioner sources, and the speed-from-power numbers depend on aerodynamics (CdA), course and heat.

## 4. Evidence gaps

- No starting FTP for him; this is the largest uncertainty in the whole report.
- No data on Goa's course profile or likely conditions (heat and wind change the split a lot).
- No research quantifying how much running volume competes with cycling progression for a lifter-triathlete.

## 6. What this means for Saumya

- **The bike is the biggest lever for the 70.3.** It's the longest leg and the best predictor of total time, and it's his least developed discipline. Every 0.1 W/kg is worth more than any swim improvement.
- **Get a real FTP early.** Even on the gym bike, a ramp test with HR gives a starting point; once he has a road bike with power, re-test properly.
- **Volume matters more than anything:** in the research, hours on the bike explain most of the difference between "untrained" and "trained" (r = 0.86). The 70.3 build should protect bike hours.
