Pair it with the planned ferritin recheck (~10–12 weeks after starting iron on Sep 15).

**Prep:**
- Fast ≥8 h, morning draw, no run beforehand.
- Tell the lab about **creatine** (raises creatinine and lowers eGFR).
- Don't clench your fist (the Sep potassium artifact).
- Skip the iron dose that morning.

## December 2026: DEXA (separate scan)

Whole-body composition + bone density, as a baseline at the start of the lean bulk (Dec 21). This is more reliable than the BIA scale while on creatine.

Options: MyDiagnostics ~₹2.5k, RxDx ~₹4k, Celara ~₹4.5k.

| DEXA metric to record | Notes |
|---|---|
| Total body fat % | Main recomposition number |
| Lean mass (total, arms, legs, trunk) | Creatine water shows up as lean mass, so compare May vs Dec while still on creatine |
| Appendicular lean mass index (ALMI) | Arms + legs lean mass / height² |
| Visceral fat (VAT) | Expected very low |
| Left/right asymmetry | Legs especially (running) |
| Bone mineral density + T/Z-score | Runner/vegetarian baseline |

## Genome (optional, one-time)

Plan: buy sequencing **with raw data** and analyse it together:
- ClinVar / ACMG secondary-findings genes
- PharmCAT drug response (CYP2C19, CYP2D6, SLCO1B1)
- HBB/G6PD variants
- APOE, only if opted in
- ancestry

**Rules:**
- Get **FASTQ + BAM + VCF** and the QC metrics. Download on day one and keep two copies.
- Confirm any actionable hit at a clinical lab (Strand / MedGenome) before acting on it.

| Option | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
