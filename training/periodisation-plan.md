# Decade Periodisation Plan — 2026 → 2036

*Written Oct 5 2026, age 22. Built on the evidence in [`research/hybrid-goals-research.md`](research/hybrid-goals-research.md) and the goal benchmarks in [`lifetime-goals.pdf`](lifetime-goals.pdf). Re-plan every December with that year's test results. Phase 1 respects the locked HIM plan and keeps run programming with Garmin Coach.*

## How to read this

- **Likely** means on track with consistent training. **Reach** means it goes right: good sleep, no injury, top-third responder.
- Running targets come from a **VDOT trajectory** (Daniels formula):
  - Likely: 36 → 41 → 43 → 48 → 51 → 53 at ages 22 → 23 → 24 → 26 → 28 → 30.
  - Reach: 36 → 43 → 46 → 52 → 55 → 57.
  - The **mile** includes your current +2.5 VDOT speed bias.
  - **Marathon times are padded**, because formula predictions run optimistic past the half (Vickers 2016).
- **Strength targets** are provisional until the first strength block gives real squat, deadlift and pull-up baselines. Bench uses your e1RM of ~91 kg.
- **One primary 95th-percentile target per phase.** Everything else gets the minimum dose to maintain it: **1–2 heavy sessions/week keeps strength** (Spiering 2021, Rønnestad 2010).

**Why this order** (from the research):
1. **Lifting comes before the speed window, not after.** Strength peaks around age 27, and heavy strength makes you faster (Llanos-Lagos 2024; Rumpf 2016). That's the research's main correction to our earlier sequence.
2. **Speed goals sit at 24–27,** matching the sprint and middle-distance peak age (~25).
3. **Road and 70.3 times come at 27–29.** You started at 22, so your endurance peak arrives later in calendar years, and the 70.3 data favours men under 30.
4. **Norseman comes last.** Ironman peaks at 32–35, and a slot takes years of ballot tickets.

**Every October: enter the Norseman ballot** (2027 ballot closes **Oct 11 2026**). Tickets build up 1 → 2 → 3 over consecutive years, and skipping a year resets them.

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Oct 2026 → Nov 2027)

**Goal:** a big aerobic base, a first 70.3 finished well, bench 100 attempted, and the sleep habit fixed. This is the phase the HIM plan already covers, so it changes scheduling only.

**Phase-wide rules** (from the research):
- **All cardio in the morning, lifting in the evening or on non-run days.** Never hard intervals and heavy legs in the same session.
- **Lower-body lifting is heavy and low-rep:** ≥80% 1RM, 3–5 reps, 4–8 sets/week. That improves running economy at the lowest fatigue cost.
- **Weekday swim on Mon or Wed morning,** never Friday (protects Friday bench).
- **One fresh-day session a week of 6–8 × 60–80 m strides or flys** keeps top speed.
- **No calorie deficit while average sleep is under ~7 h.** Target ≥7.5 h asleep (lights-out 22:30).

## Phase 2 — Strength year (Dec 2027 → Nov 2028, age 23–24)

**Why now:** strength peaks ~27, it is the most age-tolerant quality, and building it now makes the speed window faster.

| Meso | Dates | Focus | Shape | Nutrition | Targets (likely / reach) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2.1 Hypertrophy** | Dec 2027 → Mar 2028 (16 wk, 3:1) | Build muscle, especially legs and back | **4-day Posterior / Anterior / Posterior / Anterior** (the split doc's better program for off-season): 12–16 sets/muscle/wk; run 3× (25–35 km) + 1 stride session; swim 1–2 | **Lean bulk +0.25%/wk → ~80 kg (13–15%)** | Week-1 baselines: squat / deadlift / pull-up 3–5RM |

| **2.3 Cut & transition** | Aug → Nov 2028 (14 wk) | Lose the fat, rebuild running, sprint baseline v2 | Lift 3× (maintain), run 4× (35 → 50 km), sprints 1× | **Cut 0.5–0.7%/wk → 76–77 kg** | Mile **6:15–6:30 / sub-6:00**; 100 m **−2–3% vs Apr 2027** |

## Phase 3 — Speed window (Dec 2028 → Nov 2031, age 24–27)

**Primary:** mile, 5K, 400 m, 100 m. Each year is built like a track athlete's: **winter base (Dec–Mar) → spring speed (Apr–May) → summer race season (Jun–Aug) → autumn recovery or half-marathon (Sep–Nov).**

| Meso type (repeats yearly) | Focus | Shape | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|

## Testing calendar (keep these fixed)

| When | Test | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Every Oct | **Norseman ballot**; mile TT | Tickets; speed check |
| Every Dec | **Annual review**: VDOT from that year's best race vs this plan; DEXA if possible; ferritin | Re-plan |
| Every Apr | 5K TT · 30 m fly + 100 m (timed) · swim CSS | Speed and aerobic markers |
| End of strength blocks | 1RM / 3RM bench, squat, deadlift, weighted pull-up | Strength ladder |

**Decision rules at each December review:**
- **More than 2 VDOT below the likely path:** extend the current phase a year rather than skipping ahead.
- **On or above the reach path:** pull the next phase's targets forward.
- **Drawn early for Norseman (before ~2032):** decide whether to accept it as an experience race or decline (declining resets your tickets).
- **Injury or tendon flare:** cut the speed and volume targets first; strength and swimming carry on.
